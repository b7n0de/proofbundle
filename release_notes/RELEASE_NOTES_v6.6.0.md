**A feature release following 6.5.0, with the relying party's inputs for decision and outcome receipts and stricter checks across the verifiers and producers.** `decision verify` and `outcome verify` take the inputs of the offline verification of draft-gruszka-audit-decision-receipts-07, a Trust Pack can bind an executor key to the executor ids it attests, and an experimental `proofbundle scitt match` reports the axes of draft-gruszka-scitt-statement-identification-03. The command line no longer reads an abbreviated long option as the option it prefixes, so a caller that used an abbreviation must write the full option. Producers in the package now refuse, before signing, what their own verifiers would refuse over budget or at the wrong signature length; the CHANGELOG names each case and what stays with the verifier.

```
python -m pip install --upgrade proofbundle==6.6.0
```

## Highlights

- **`decision verify` and `outcome verify` take the inputs the relying party supplies.** `decision verify` checks the action bytes (`--action`), the case the relying party chooses (`--case approval` with `--approval`, or `--case declined`) and a consumption state it supplies (`--consumed`, `--received`), which proofbundle reads and never writes. `outcome verify` takes the expected executor, the effect bytes, the bound decision with its key and the evaluation time, and checks the executed case. Without an input its check is not performed, and these verifiers are not a complete implementation of the draft's procedure.
- **A Trust Pack key attests only the executor ids its pack names.** A key entry may carry `executorIds`. An outcome under another identifier is not trusted (`EXECUTOR_ID_NOT_ATTESTED`), and a key entry without `executorIds` binds no identifier and says so (`EXECUTOR_ID_NOT_BOUND`). The schema states the bounds the validator holds on `keys`, `keyIds` and `executorIds`.
- **Stricter command line and binding checks.** Every parser refuses an abbreviated long option with exit 2 instead of reading it as the option it prefixes. `decision verify`, `outcome verify` and `relation-statement verify` fail an attached `--with-related` target that does not verify or whose predicate fails the structure check of its type, also where no edge of the receipt names it, and refuse a `--related-pub` without its target.
- **Producers write only what their verifiers read, and five containers refuse members they do not name.** The SD-JWT producers, `emit_bundle` and `dsse.sign_envelope` read what they sign as their verifiers will, and every producer of an Ed25519 signature writes a signer's output only at 64 bytes. The pre-tag receipt readers, `verify_evidence_pack`, `measure_vector_set`, row C10.4 of the audit matrix and `measure_build` each judge a closed set of members and refuse or name a member outside it.

## What a receipt still does not prove

A valid signature binds the signed content to the signing key. It does not make a reported result true.

`decision verify` and `outcome verify` perform the steps of draft-gruszka-audit-decision-receipts only where their inputs are supplied, and they are not a complete implementation of the draft's procedure. Section 3.1 requires the expected `executor.id` and, for Case 1, the approval record bytes, so a pass without them is not a pass of the draft's procedure. Even with every input supplied, a receipt the draft rejects can pass them, for example with an evaluation time before `decidedAt` outside Case 1. [What proofbundle does not claim](https://github.com/b7n0de/proofbundle/blob/057b1a809ca89a79168927e5e18d0ab77238704c/docs/NON_CLAIMS.md#steps-of-draft-gruszka-audit-decision-receipts-that-proofbundle-performs-only-with-their-inputs) lists measured examples.

proofbundle reads a supplied consumption state and never writes it. Honouring an approval at most once stays with the relying party. An identifier a Trust Pack names in `executorIds` is a permission the pack grants to the key, not proof that the signer is that executor.

### Added

- **`proofbundle scitt match` (EXPERIMENTAL) reports the axes of Section 6 of draft-gruszka-scitt-statement-identification-03 for a reference and a candidate COSE_Sign1.** You name the scheme, `ToBeSigned` (Section 5.1, with `--external-aad` and `--detached-payload`) or `WholeBytes` (the digest of the candidate bytes as given, a local experimental scheme), and its hash function; there is no fallback to another scheme. The result gives `ref_usable`, `match` and `is_scitt` as the section defines them, and a `signature` axis under the Ed25519 profile of SPEC.md section 4a; `sig_valid` is reported as not evaluated, because this reader does not apply rule 2 of the section (A and R of order L). Exit 0 is MATCH and nothing else, 1 NO_MATCH, 3 INDETERMINATE, 2 an input that cannot be read or input the reader does not read, which gets no verdict. No verify path imports the reader. `tests/test_scitt_match_reader.py` runs the 14 cases of `interop/scitt-statement-identification-03` and their variants through the command line, the counter-cases for missing inputs and for the scheme, the two inputs where `signature` and the draft's `sig_valid` differ, and single-byte mutants of three candidates against the checker of that folder (docs/SCITT_STATEMENT_MATCH.md).
- **`_strict_cbor` says which refusals are no statement about validity.** `CborUnrepresentable` marks valid input with a map key the reader does not represent (an array, a map, a tag or a NaN as key, and two distinct keys that are one Python dict key, such as 1 and true or 0 and 0.0), `CborBeyondLimits` input over its byte, depth or width budget. Both are subclasses of `CborInvalid` with the same reasons as before, so every existing caller reads them unchanged (`tests/test_strict_cbor_refusal_kinds.py`).
- **`decision verify` checks the action bytes, the case the relying party chooses and a consumption state it supplies (draft-gruszka-audit-decision-receipts, step 10 of Section 3.2, Section 3.3 and Section 4).** `--action PATH` (`action_bytes`): the SHA-256 of the bytes must equal `proposedAction.parametersDigest`; a mismatch, or a receipt without that digest, exits 2. `--case approval` with `--approval PATH`, or `--case declined` (`case`, `approval_record`): the receipt must hold under the chosen case, the receipt never chooses it; a case that does not hold exits 2. `--consumed PATH` and `--received PATH` (`consumed_state`, `received_copies`) are read only. With the approval case, an approval whose identity (approvalId, actionDigest and session) is in any generation of the state, a state holding an entry that is no such identity, a received copy without an identity or a readable validUntil, and an evaluation time at or after the earliest validUntil of the copies of that approval exit 3. proofbundle never writes the state and never marks an approval consumed; an OK says only that the identity is not in the state given.
- **`outcome verify` takes the inputs of Section 3.4 and checks Case 3 of Section 3.3.** `--executor-id` (`expected_executor_id`), `--effect PATH` (`effect_bytes`), `--decision PATH` with `--decision-pub` (`decision_envelope`, `decision_public_key`), `--verification-time` (`now`) and `--case executed` (`case`). The bound decision must pass `decision verify` under its key at the evaluation time, strict, with a derived subject and its freshness included, and its decisionMaker.id must be the `--decision-maker-id` the relying party expects. Then the outcome must name its content root and its parametersDigest, the keys and the identities must differ, and decidedAt <= performedAt < validity.expiresAt of the decision must hold. Case executed asks, before the status, for the outcome's structure, a subject that is the digest of the predicate, the bound decision, the expected executor, a non-null effectDigest on an executed outcome, and matching effect bytes where given. Each failure exits 2. Rules of the draft that `decision verify` and `outcome verify` do not apply are not applied here either; docs/NON_CLAIMS.md gives measured examples. `--verification-time` takes a whole past second, as on `decision verify`.

A signer that returns a real Ed25519 signature in a buffer, as bytes, a `bytearray`, a `memoryview` (also one that is not contiguous) or an `array.array`, writes what it wrote before. An output that exports no buffer, such as a list of byte values or an object that only answers `bytes()`, is refused; the archive timestamp took both before.
