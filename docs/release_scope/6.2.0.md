# Release scope — 6.2.0 (in progress)

This file exists because of a cut, not because of a plan. Owner word of 2026-09-19, order
`QITEM-PROOFBUNDLE-610-SCHNITT-LANDEN-KETTE-01`, option A: 6.1.0 is the substance that has been on
`main` since `v6.0.0`, plus the two P1 findings of the 2026-09-19 audit, R7 and the cheap
documentation findings. The remaining lines of the scope of 2026-09-12 move here unchanged.

**Nothing here is released, and nothing here is started.** The lines below are taken from
`docs/release_scope/6.1.0.md` at `79f66a2`, word for word, so that the comparison between what was
promised and what arrives stays possible without anyone having to remember it.

## Why the moved lines are still in German

The order says they move **unchanged**, and byte equality was checked rather than assumed: 54
expected, 54 present, none missing, none foreign. The owner decision of 2026-09-19 that new and
re-cast files are English applies to the prose of this file, which is English. The table rows are a
verbatim quotation of an existing artefact, and rewriting a quotation would make the byte-equality
check above meaningless. They keep their wording until the line itself is taken up, at which point
its new home is written in English.

## Why moved and not dropped

Measured on 2026-09-19: the 6.1.0 scope carried 56 lines, and **two** of them are delivered — P19
(PR 224, `9fdedad`) and R7 (this cut). The owner names the same order of magnitude from a different
source, the landing card: one of 47. Two independent derivations, one statement.

At today's rate the remaining 54 lines are weeks. The cut is therefore not a judgement on those
lines, it is the decision to stop 6.1.0 waiting for them. `RESTRISIKO_610.md` names every one of
them as open; they leave this version, not the books.

**The owner cards `OA-f7e867ec7e`, `OA-03b1f66681` and `OA-b482712050` of 2026-09-12 are re-cast by
this.** That is an owner word of 2026-09-19 and not a contradiction for a reader to resolve.

## Honest limit of this file

The branch names in the tables below are the names from the E1 sheet of 2026-09-12. **None of them
exists**, and measured on 2026-09-19 exactly one of the 34 pull requests landed since `v6.0.0`
carries one. They are a suggestion for how the work could be named, not a mapping any tool can read.
Whoever takes up one of these lines picks their own branch name and records it here.
