## Why these lines are here and not in 6.2.0

6.2.0 is cut against the four outcomes: E1 a second implementation, E2 in-toto PR 575, E3 three
external users, E4 adoption into a specification. A line belongs there when it brings one of them
closer, closes a P1 of the audit of 2026-09-19, or closes a place where the verifier says more than
it checks.

The lines below do none of those. Each one carries the sentence saying so, as the release rule of
2026-09-09 requires: a register line per move, not a bulk transfer. They are open, not dismissed;
`RESTRISIKO_610.md` names them and continues to.
