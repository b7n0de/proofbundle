NOT a released version and it is NOT a pre-tag audit of one: no tag `v6.1.0`
exists, no receipt was produced here, and the carrier states its own signature state as

for, and does that entry exist. The answer is the carrier below. Everything in it is either cut out
of a named source at a named byte range, carried over from the signed v1 register, or marked as a

## How a title relates to its source

A title here is DERIVED, not quoted verbatim, and the rule differs per find form. The carrier
declares the rule of every form it used under `language_scope.title_derivation`; the form of a
single record stands in `evidence[].fundart`. Applying the rule of that form to the bytes of that
record's evidence reproduces its title exactly, and
`tests/test_die_sprachangabe_deckt_was_sie_sagt.py` re-derives all of them rather than believing
the field.

- `prosa_zusage` — the paragraph is flattened to single spaces and its FIRST SENTENCE is taken; a sentence longer than 200 characters is cut at a word boundary and marked with a trailing ellipsis
